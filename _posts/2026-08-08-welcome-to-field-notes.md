It was time for a refresh.

After running brettwagner.me on a Jekyll theme for several years, I decided it was time for a refresh. Same name and purpose, just rebuilt feel and some backend tweaks (thanks Claude!). *Field Notes* is a place for dispatches from ordinary life: what I'm reading, what I'm thinking about, and the occasional polished article.

The design goal was to get out of the way of the words. I liked the simplicity of the [Hitchens theme](https://patdryburgh.github.io/hitchens/), but wanted something a little simpler with some other custom tweaks. It is another static site hosted on GitHub Pages, which means I can write and update in Markdown with very little friction.

If you'd like to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/feed.xml) and a
[JSON feed](/feed.json). Otherwise, wander in whenever you like. Older entries
live [under their tags](/tags/).
