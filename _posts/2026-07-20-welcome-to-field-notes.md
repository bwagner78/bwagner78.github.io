It was time for a refresh.

After running brettwagner.me on a Jekyll theme for several years, I decided it was time for a refresh. Same name and purpose, just rebuilt feel and some backend tweaks (thanks Claude!). *Field Notes* is a place for dispatches from ordinary life:
what I'm reading, what I'm thinking about, and the occasional finished piece.

The design goal was to get out of the way of the words. Aged paper, a serif
font for easy reading, and just enough structure to find your way around. No
pop-ups, no share bars, no infinite scroll, just words.

If you'd like to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/feed.xml) and a
[JSON feed](/feed.json). Otherwise, wander in whenever you like. Older entries
live [under their tags](/tags/).
